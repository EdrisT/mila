# Mila 1.9.8

**Fixes**

- **Mila no longer quits when you stop a recording.** In 1.9.7, pressing Stop with speaker labels on could close Mila without warning. Your recording was kept and finished transcribing on the next launch, but the app should never have closed. It now stops normally, even when the speaker-detection helper has failed in the background.
- **Speaker detection works again.** 1.9.7 shipped with a mismatched Python component, so no speakers were detected: there were no live speaker labels during a recording and no speaker separation after it. Speaker labels, known-speaker recognition and speaker name suggestions all work again. Nothing to reinstall: update Mila, and the next recording is diarized as before.
- **AI summaries are now something you ask for, not something that happens.** Until now Mila summarized every finished recording by itself — twice, once when the transcript landed and again when speaker identification finished, so a meeting cost two calls to your provider and threw the first summary away. Nothing is sent on its own any more. Each recording you haven't summarized shows a **Summarize** button (and a right-click **Summarize** in the list); one you've summarized gets **Regenerate summary**. Live AI is unchanged: it still summarizes continuously while you record and finalizes when you press Stop.
- **A summary you already have is never thrown away by a re-transcription.** It used to be deleted and replaced by a fresh call. Now the overview stays and Mila says "this summary was written before the transcript was re-transcribed" next to it, with a **Regenerate** button if you want a new one.
